**Last updated: 4 September 2026**

This policy explains what personal data Heapforge Ltd. handles in connection with the **heapforge.com** website, why we handle it, and what rights you have over it.

The **Heapforge Reader** and **Heapforge Journal** apps are covered by separate privacy policies: [Reader](/legal/heapforge-reader-privacy-policy/), [Journal](/legal/heapforge-journal-privacy-policy/).

## In short

heapforge.com is a static website. It sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics**, embeds **no tracking pixels or advertising**, and has **no user accounts**. There is nothing to consent to, which is why you will not see a cookie banner.

The only personal data that reaches us is what our hosting provider records automatically to serve and protect the site, plus whatever you choose to send us by email or post.

## What we collect, why, and for how long

### Hosting and security logs

heapforge.com is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**. To deliver the site and to protect it from abuse, Cloudflare automatically records technical request data on our behalf.

- **Data:** IP address, user-agent string, requested URL, referring URL, timestamp, response status, and approximate country derived from the IP address.
- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) — delivering the website reliably and defending it against attack, fraud and abuse. We consider this proportionate because the data is technical, is not used to build profiles, and is not combined with anything else.
- **Retention:** kept only for the period applied by Cloudflare's edge logging, which is short-term and measured in days. We do not export these logs into any long-term store of our own.

### Email correspondence

If you write to us — for example to **contact@heapforge.com** or **privacy@heapforge.com** — we receive and keep your message.

- **Data:** your email address, any name or signature you include, and the content of your message and our replies.
- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), in reading and answering messages sent to us; Article 6(1)(b) where the exchange concerns a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering one; and Article 6(1)(c) where we are legally required to act, such as when you exercise a data protection right.
- **Retention:** correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with the matter and then for up to **24 months**, after which it is deleted. Records we must keep for a longer statutory period — for example accounting records, or a log of data protection requests and how we handled them — are kept for that period instead.

### Postal mail

If you write to our registered address, we receive whatever you send.

- **Data:** your name, return address, and the content of your letter.
- **Lawful basis:** as for email correspondence above.
- **Retention:** as for email correspondence above.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We set no cookies and use no local storage, session storage, fingerprinting or similar tracking technologies on heapforge.com. The site loads a web font from Google Fonts; when it does, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which will see your IP address and user agent as part of serving that font file. No cookie is set by that request.

## Who your data is shared with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising or profiling. Personal data is handled on our behalf by the following processors, each under a written data processing agreement:

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — website hosting, content delivery, and security. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC** — Google Workspace, which hosts our email. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may also disclose personal data where we are required to do so by law, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it;
- have inaccurate data **rectified** and incomplete data completed;
- have your data **erased** in the circumstances set out in Article 17;
- **restrict** our processing in the circumstances set out in Article 18;
- receive data you provided to us in a portable format, and have it **transmitted** to another controller, where Article 20 applies;
- **object** to processing carried out on the basis of our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent. We do not currently rely on consent for anything described here.

We carry out no automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these rights, email **[privacy@heapforge.com](mailto:privacy@heapforge.com)** or write to the registered address above. We will respond within **one month**. That period can be extended by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we will tell you within the first month and explain why. Exercising these rights is free; we may charge a reasonable fee, or refuse, only where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

We may ask for enough information to satisfy ourselves of your identity before acting on a request.

## Complaints

If you think we have handled your personal data improperly, please tell us first so we can put it right.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority:

- in the **United Kingdom**, the Information Commissioner's Office — [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), helpline 0303 123 1113;
- in the **EU/EEA**, the supervisory authority of the country where you live, work, or where you believe the problem occurred — in Bulgaria, for example, the Commission for Personal Data Protection ([cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg/)).
