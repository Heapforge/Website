**Last updated:** 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Heapforge Ltd.** ("Heapforge", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use **Heapforge Reader** (the "app"), or when you otherwise contact us about it. It also explains the rights you have and how to exercise them.

The **Heapforge Journal**, **Heapforge Markdown** and **Heapforge HexEditor** apps are covered by a [separate privacy policy](/legal/heapforge-journal-privacy-policy/), and the heapforge.com website by [another](/legal/heapforge-com-privacy-policy/).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to personal data processed through the app, as well as when you communicate with us. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we link to, which have their own privacy policies.

The app uses services provided by Google and Apple **for purchase verification only**. Those companies process the data involved under their own terms and privacy policies, as described in [section 5.1](#51-google-and-apple-purchase-verification-only).

## 3. Personal data we collect

We try to collect only what we need. **We do not operate any servers for the app**, so the app sends nothing to Heapforge. Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

- Contact information - such as your name and email address, when you email us or otherwise get in touch.
- Communications - the content of messages you send us, including any attachments, and our replies.
- Transaction information - purchases are handled entirely by the store you buy from. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying sales reports (see [section 5.1.3](#513-what-we-can-see)), and we do not directly collect or store payment card details.
- Any other information you choose to provide to us.

The app does not send technical or usage information to us.

We do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health, religious or biometric data). Please do not send us such information unless we have specifically asked for it.

### 3.1 What stays on your device

Everything below is stored locally, in the app's own private storage on your device. It is never sent to Heapforge or to any third party:

- the books, documents and other files you add to the app, and their contents;
- your library: titles, authors, covers, collections, tags and other metadata;
- your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, annotations and notes;
- your appearance and reading preferences, such as theme, typeface and margins;
- search history within the app, and any local cache the app builds to render your files quickly.

This data is removed when you delete the app, or when you clear its data through your device's settings.

**Device backups.** If you have turned on your operating system's backup service, such as Android Auto Backup, Google One or iCloud Backup, your device may include the app's local data in that backup. That copy is made by your device and governed by [Google's](https://policies.google.com/privacy) or [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) privacy policy, not by ours. Heapforge has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device settings.

## 4. How we use personal data and our lawful bases

Under the UK and EU GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for each use of your personal data.

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| :---- | :---- |
| Delivering the app and its features to you | Contract (the licence under which the app is supplied to you) |
| Verifying that your copy of the app was legitimately purchased | Contract; legitimate interests (preventing unauthorised copying and distribution of software we sell) |
| Responding to your emails and enquiries | Legitimate interests (communicating with people who contact us); contract, where the enquiry relates to the app |
| Keeping business records and meeting legal, tax and regulatory obligations | Legal obligation |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have considered whether those interests are outweighed by your rights and freedoms. You can contact us for more information about this balancing.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Third-party service providers

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our business. They are listed below.

### 5.1 Google and Apple (purchase verification only)

Heapforge Reader is a paid app. To confirm that your copy was legitimately purchased, and so to protect the app against unauthorised copying and distribution, the app checks your entitlement with the store you installed it from. **This is the only thing the app uses Google and Apple services for.**

Google and Apple are **not** our processors for this. Each processes the data involved as an **independent controller**, under its own terms and privacy policy, in connection with the account you hold with it. The check happens directly between your device and the store; nothing passes through Heapforge, and nothing from your library is involved.

#### 5.1.1 Google Play

If you use the app on Android, it uses **Google Play Services** and the **Google Play Billing Library** provided by **Google** to confirm that your Google account is entitled to the app. In carrying out that check, Google processes data such as your Google account identifier, the purchase token for your order, and information about your device and the installed app.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy ([https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) and the Google Play Terms of Service ([https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/play-terms/](https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/play-terms/)).

#### 5.1.2 Apple App Store

If you use the app on an Apple device, it uses **StoreKit** and App Store receipt validation provided by **Apple** to confirm that your Apple Account is entitled to the app. Apple processes the associated purchase and device information.

For more information, see the Apple Privacy Policy ([https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)).

#### 5.1.3 What we can see

**We do not collect any identifiable personal data from Google or Apple.** We receive only the aggregated, non-identifying sales reports that every developer sees in the Google Play Console and App Store Connect, such as unit counts, revenue, refunds, and broad country and device breakdowns. We do not receive, and cannot request, the identity of an individual purchaser.

### 5.2 Google Workspace

We use **Google Workspace**, provided by Google, as our email provider. When you email us, for example for support with the app, your message - including your name, email address, message content, attachments and related metadata - is received, stored and processed on Google's systems. Google processes this data on our behalf under its business data processing terms and does not use Workspace customer data for advertising.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy ([https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) and Google Cloud Privacy Notice ([https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice](https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice)).

### 5.3 Other recipients

We may also share some personal data with:

- professional advisers such as accountants, lawyers and insurers, where necessary;
- law enforcement, regulators, courts or other authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public;
- a buyer, successor or investor in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

## 6. What the app does not do

The app contains:

- no analytics or usage telemetry sent to us or to any third-party service;
- no crash or diagnostic reporting sent to us or to any third-party service;
- no advertising, ad SDKs or advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites;
- no Heapforge accounts, sign-in or cloud sync, and no Heapforge servers;
- no sale or sharing of personal data by Heapforge.

## 7. Permissions

The app asks for access to files and storage only so that it can open the books and documents **you choose to open**. Reading, indexing and rendering those files happens entirely on your device. The app does not scan your storage for unrelated content and does not upload anything you open.

Network access is used only for the purchase verification described in [section 5.1](#51-google-and-apple-purchase-verification-only).

## 11. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. As a general guide:

- Email correspondence - kept for 24 months after our last contact, unless it relates to an ongoing matter or we need to keep it longer for legal reasons.
- Business and transaction records - kept for as long as required by law (in the UK, typically up to six years after the end of the relevant financial year).
- Data on your device - kept until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- Purchase records held by Google or Apple - kept under their own policies. Heapforge does not hold a copy.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

## 12. How we protect personal data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include encryption in transit, access controls and choosing reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 13. Your rights (UK, EU, EEA and Switzerland)

If you are in the UK, EU, EEA or Switzerland, you have the following rights, subject to certain conditions and exceptions:

- Access - to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- Rectification - to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Erasure - to ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances.
- Restriction - to ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
- Data portability - to receive data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format, or have it transferred to another organisation.
- Objection - to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to object at any time to direct marketing.
- Withdraw consent - where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing.
- Not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [privacy@heapforge.com](mailto:privacy@heapforge.com). We will respond within one month, which may be extended by up to two further months for complex requests. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Exercising your rights is usually free of charge.

Because the app sends nothing to Heapforge, a request about your use of the app will usually show that we hold no data about you, other than any emails you have sent us. We will confirm this in writing so you have a record of it.
