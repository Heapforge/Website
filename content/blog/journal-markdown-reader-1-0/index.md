Today we're releasing version 1.0 of three apps: **Heapforge Journal**,
**Heapforge Markdown** and **Heapforge Reader**. They share a few principles.
They work offline and keep your files on your device. They don't need an
account and they don't track you. Each one tries to do one job well.

All three are **available on Google Play now**. Desktop builds and App Store
releases for iPhone and iPad are coming soon. More on that
[at the end](#availability).

## Heapforge Journal

{{< app-card name="Heapforge Journal" icon="journal/icon.png"
    tagline="A private journal where you design the page you fill in each day."
    play="com.heapforge.journal" site="https://journal.heapforge.com" >}}

Most journalling apps give you a blank box or a fixed template. Journal lets
you build the page yourself, then fill it in every day.

- **Design your own form.** Mix text, sketch and photo sections in any order,
  each with its own title and heading size. Change the layout whenever you
  like. Entries you've already written keep the layout they were written in.
- **Write with Markdown.** A formatting bar handles bold, headings, lists and
  quotes. Entries save as you type, can be left as drafts and can be backdated
  to the day they belong to.
- **A sketchbook in every entry.** Use pencil, pen and crayon in any colour on
  a canvas that grows as you draw. With a stylus, lines follow pressure and
  tilt, and the eraser end rubs out. Sketches stay editable.
- **Photos and videos** in every entry, which open full screen and zoom right
  in.
- **Mood calendar.** Pick an emoji for each day, add a note if you want to
  remember why, and see the whole month at a glance.
- **Streaks and writing sessions.** Set aside time to write and Journal reminds
  you 15 minutes before each session. Streak reminders stop once you've written
  for the day.
- **Export anywhere.** Export any entry as a PDF, an editable Word document or
  Markdown, with its pictures included.
- **For your eyes only.** You can require your fingerprint, face or screen lock
  to open Journal. Everything stays on your device: there's no account, no
  upload and no analytics.

## Heapforge Markdown

{{< app-card name="Heapforge Markdown" icon="markdown/icon.png"
    tagline="Write in plain text. Read it beautifully."
    play="com.heapforge.markdown" site="https://markdown.heapforge.com" >}}

Markdown is a fast, focused editor for Markdown, LaTeX, AsciiDoc,
reStructuredText and wikitext. You write the source, see it rendered, and
every note stays a plain file you own.

- **Every Markdown flavour in one.** It reads CommonMark plus GitHub, GitLab,
  Pandoc, MultiMarkdown and Markdown Extra syntax. Task lists can be ticked
  straight from the preview.
- **Maths that looks like maths.** Write LaTeX maths inline or on its own line
  and get properly typeset equations in the preview.
- **Full LaTeX documents.** Open any `.tex` file to preview its sections,
  theorems, tables and equations, with highlighting for every command as you
  edit.
- **AsciiDoc, reStructuredText and wikitext** each get a live preview, their
  own insert toolbar and full export. Any other text file opens too, and its
  encoding and line endings are kept.
- **Notes as plain files.** Notes live in folders of ordinary files, not a
  database, so nothing is locked into the app. You can search names and
  contents across all of them.
- **Locked notes.** Locked notes are encrypted with AES-256 and open with your
  fingerprint or screen lock.
- **Edit, Preview or Split.** Switch views on any screen. On wide screens, edit
  and preview side by side. The insert toolbar writes tables, links and blocks
  for you, and find and replace supports regular expressions.
- **Export to anything.** Export to Markdown, plain text, HTML, Word and PDF,
  all made on your device and offline.

## Heapforge Reader

{{< app-card name="Heapforge Reader" icon="reader/icon.png"
    tagline="A focused e-book, PDF and comic reader with bite-sized pages."
    play="com.heapforge.reader" site="https://reader.heapforge.com" >}}

Reader is built around one idea: a page should hold one thought. Books are
split into cards you read one at a time, and the app helps you make reading a
daily habit.

- **Bite-sized pages.** Each card holds a single thought, set in a typeface
  made for comfortable reading. There's no scrolling and no walls of text.
- **Fixation reading.** The opening letters of every word are set heavier, so
  your eye skips ahead without losing its place on the line. Choose a strength
  from Off to Strong.
- **Turn pages like a deck of cards.** Swipe and the card lifts away to reveal
  the next one, or tap either side of the page.
- **Bring the books you already own.** Reader opens EPUB, PDF, MOBI and AZW,
  RTF, CHM, FB2 and CBZ/CBR comics. Files import straight from your device,
  with no conversion, no account and no uploads.
- **A streak worth keeping.** Meet your daily goal and the streak grows, with
  streak freezes for the days life gets in the way. A year of activity, plus
  your minutes read per day, week, month or year, are always one tap away.
- **A reading schedule.** Plan when you read and get a heads-up fifteen minutes
  before each session.
- **Make it comfortable.** Choose from six typefaces, three sizes and light or
  dark themes. Pages are re-measured when you change them, and you never lose
  your place.
